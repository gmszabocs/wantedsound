    ALL EARS.

    The world’s making a right lovely racket.
    Someone ought to record it.

    Wanted Sound is Ben Scott’s collection of field recordings, based in Miskolc, Hungary. It’s a sonic record of places, objects, natural phenomena and the things people get up to. Some days, that means waves giving the Croatian coast a proper battering. Other days, it’s the hum of a bus, a creaking pine or an old turntable having a moment.

    The recordings are kept raw and unprocessed. There’s character in the rough edges, and we’re rather fond of it. This little listening room offers a handful of excerpts from the archive. The full recordings live on Bandcamp, ready for a deeper listen. Stick your headphones on and give the everyday a wee bit of attention.
